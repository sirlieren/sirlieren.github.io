From harvest
to production

I am developing Oddroot Farm solo for PC. The current playable prototype connects a grid-based farm to crop growth, daily energy, barn inventory and machine production.

Harvest goes into the barn rather than being sold automatically. Crops can be sold for immediate cash or reserved as ingredients. Machines turn wheat into bread, corn into popcorn and sugar cane into sugar; production occupies slots over multiple game days, and finished goods remain in their slots until collected.

Each day begins with a choice of one of three upgrades. A weekly tribute creates an ongoing budget constraint: investing too much in production can leave the player unable to pay when the week ends.

Implementation
focus

  • Farm state: soil preparation, watering and crop age are tracked separately, supporting crops with different growth stages.
  • Day-based coordination: the calendar drives crop growth, machine progress, daily energy and weekly payments.
  • Inventory and production: shared barn stock supplies machine recipes, with construction, production slots and collection represented as distinct states.
  • Mouse interaction: a drag keeps the initial action and selected crop, processes each eligible plot once and checks money and energy per plot. Clicks that begin over UI do not act on the farm.

Development
direction

The implemented core covers farming, storage, sales, machine processing, daily upgrade selection and losing a run when the weekly tribute cannot be paid.

Planned additions include product orders and threats announced several days ahead, with protections purchased using gold. These systems are not yet implemented. Recipe unlocks, long-run progression and the balance between investment, tribute and protection are still being designed.

The intended experience pairs the satisfaction of building a productive farm with strategic pressure, without horror elements. Visual direction is still being developed; gameplay media will be added as the project progresses.
