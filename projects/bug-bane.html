What I built

My focus was building gameplay that could support fast, readable combat and a range of encounters. The project gave me ownership of the full development loop: implementing systems, tuning them in play, then preparing updates and the Steam release.

  • Built expressive combat feedback through lively animations, satisfying impacts and readable enemy reactions.
  • Shaped the game feel so attacks, hits and enemy encounters feel responsive and rewarding.
  • Contributed to the game's humorous tone through its playful combat presentation.
  • Developed gameplay systems and handled demo distribution, updates and release work.

Project snapshot

Players manually aim, combine items into builds, and take on objectives across distinct zones. Its lively combat animation and playful tone support the moment-to-moment feel. The demo reached more than 1,500 users, and the game entered Steam Early Access in January 2026.

Those release and reach figures are from my CV; the gameplay description and current store status are from the Steam listing.
