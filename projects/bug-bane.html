Survivors

A solo-developed action roguelike where a mutant plant fights back against bug swarms. Two systems shape the run: temporary mutations dropped by enemies, and a memory-based chest choice when leveling up.

Mutation drops

Defeated enemies drop mutations like gold. Contact starts the effect, and only one mutation can be active at a time. Its beneficial or harmful nature is unknown until pickup, so every drop presents a quick risk decision in the middle of combat.

  • Rapid fire: greatly increases fire rate for 7–10 seconds.
  • Bamboo volley: bamboo pieces fire around the player and hit enemies for 5–8 seconds.
  • Salt rain: falling salt fragments damage the player for 5–6 seconds.
  • Bad weather: adverse weather and intense screen shake last 5–6 seconds.

Mutation definitions use Unity ScriptableObjects, making it straightforward to add content from the existing set of effects. A genuinely new effect or mechanic still requires implementing that behavior in code.

As the solo developer, I also handled the game’s demo distribution, updates and Steam Early Access release.

Memory chest

A small icon below the XP bar acts as the key to a later reward. At higher levels, the icon disappears at a lower XP-bar fill threshold, asking the player to remember it. On level-up, three chests marked with icons appear; the player has roughly 3–6 seconds to click a chest repeatedly and break it, with less time as progression increases.

Matching the remembered icon opens three stronger item options, from which the player chooses one. Choosing the wrong icon still offers three random items and a choice of one. If the chest is not broken before time runs out, there is no reward. The usual three-item selection therefore remains, preceded by memory and a timed interaction.

Icon visibility and timer duration are the two difficulty parameters: both affect how much time the player has to remember and act.

Project snapshot

Players manually aim, combine items into builds, and take on objectives across distinct zones. Its lively combat animation and playful tone support the moment-to-moment feel. The demo reached more than 1,500 users, and the game entered Steam Early Access in January 2026.
